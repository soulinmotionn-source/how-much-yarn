How Much Yarn for a Baby Sweater?

Project size: Baby sweater, 12-month size

Advertisement
Quick answer: A 12-month baby sweater takes about 600 yards of worsted-weight yarn — roughly 3 skeins of a standard 220-yard skein with buffer. In DK, plan on about 700 yards; in fingering, about 800.

Baby sweaters are wonderful gift projects: quick, small enough for precious yarns, and they use soft washable fibers that new parents appreciate.

Frequently asked questions

How many skeins for a baby sweater?

Usually 2 to 3 skeins of worsted (220-yard skeins) for a 12-month size. Newborn sizes need about 2 skeins; 2T sizes need about 3 to 4.

What is the best yarn for baby clothes?

Soft, machine-washable yarns: superwash merino, cotton blends, or premium acrylics. Avoid anything scratchy — baby skin is sensitive.

How much yarn for a newborn sweater?

About 350–450 yards of worsted — roughly 2 skeins. Newborn garments are delightfully quick.

Can I use leftover yarn for a baby sweater?

Yes, if you have enough of one dye lot for the body. Stripes are a charming way to combine smaller leftovers.
