How Much Yarn for a Hat?

Project size: Adult hat: ~22″ head circumference

Advertisement
Quick answer: An adult hat takes about 200 yards of worsted-weight yarn — a single skein with room to spare. In fingering weight, plan on about 300 yards; in bulky, about 160 yards.

Hats are the ultimate stash-buster: most use well under one full skein, which is why so many makers keep a “hat drawer” of gift-ready projects.

Frequently asked questions

How many skeins for a beanie?

One. A single 220-yard worsted skein makes an adult beanie with yarn left over. Even a slouchy or cabled beanie rarely needs more than one skein.

How much yarn for a baby hat?

About 100–150 yards of worsted — less than one skein. Baby hats are perfect for using up leftover yarn.

Does a pom-pom add much yarn?

A full pom-pom uses roughly 20–30 yards. It's already covered by the calculator's buffer in most cases.

How much yarn for a winter hat with a folded brim?

Add about 25% to the standard estimate — roughly 250 yards of worsted for an adult folded-brim hat.
