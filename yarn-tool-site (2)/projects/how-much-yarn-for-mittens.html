How Much Yarn for Mittens?

Project size: Adult pair

Advertisement
Quick answer: An adult pair of mittens takes about 180 yards of worsted-weight yarn — well under one skein. In fingering weight, plan on about 250 yards.

Gloves and fingerless mitts use a similar amount; full gloves with individual fingers use slightly more than mittens.

Frequently asked questions

How many skeins for mittens?

One skein is plenty for most adult mittens — you'll have leftovers. Even two pairs often come from a single 220-yard worsted skein.

Mittens or gloves — which uses more yarn?

Gloves use a bit more because of the individual fingers. Add roughly 15–20% to mitten yardage for gloves.

How much yarn for fingerless mitts?

About 100–150 yards of worsted — a quick one-evening project and a great stash-buster.

What yarn weight is best for warm mittens?

Worsted or bulky wool (or wool blends) — the thicker fabric and wool's insulation make the warmest mittens.
