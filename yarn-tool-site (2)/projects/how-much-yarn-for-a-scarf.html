How Much Yarn for a Scarf?

Project size: Average scarf: 8″ × 60″ (20 × 152 cm)

Advertisement
Quick answer: An average 8″ × 60″ scarf takes about 350 yards of worsted-weight yarn — usually 2 skeins of a standard 220-yard skein. A long or wide statement scarf can need 500+ yards, while a short neck scarf might need only 200.

Scarves are the classic stash-buster and one-skein project: one 400-yard skein of fingering or sock yarn makes a beautiful lightweight scarf or shawlette.

Frequently asked questions

How many skeins of yarn for a scarf?

Usually 1 to 2 skeins. Two 220-yard worsted skeins comfortably make an average scarf with buffer; one 400+ yard skein of fingering or DK makes a lighter scarf.

How much yarn for a chunky knit scarf?

About 300 yards of bulky or 225 yards of super bulky for an average scarf — typically 2 to 3 skeins of bulky or 3 to 4 skeins of super bulky.

What is a good scarf length?

60 inches (152 cm) is the standard adult length, not counting fringe. 70–72 inches suits taller people or a wrapped style.

Does crochet use more yarn for scarves?

Yes — crochet uses roughly 25–30% more yarn than knitting for the same scarf. Add about a quarter to knit estimates when crocheting.
