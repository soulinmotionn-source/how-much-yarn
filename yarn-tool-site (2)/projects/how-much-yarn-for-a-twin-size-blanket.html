How Much Yarn for a Twin Size Blanket?

Project size: Twin blanket: 66″ × 90″ (168 × 229 cm)

Advertisement
Quick answer: A twin-size blanket (about 66″ × 90″) needs roughly 3,200 yards of worsted-weight yarn — about 15 skeins of a standard 220-yard skein with buffer. In DK weight, plan on about 4,000 yards; in bulky, about 2,400 yards.

Twin blankets are a sweet spot: big enough for a bed, but still a feasible handmade project. Many makers use this size for dorm-room or guest-room blankets.

Frequently asked questions

How many skeins for a twin blanket?

In worsted weight, about 15 skeins of a standard 220-yard skein (with buffer). In bulky, roughly 20 skeins of 120-yard skeins. Always round up and buy one extra.

What is a good size for a twin crochet blanket?

66″ × 90″ is the standard twin blanket size. For a little extra drape over the sides of the bed, some makers go 70″ × 90″.

How long does a twin blanket take to crochet?

In worsted weight with a simple stitch, expect 40–60 hours of work. In bulky or super bulky, that can drop to 20–30 hours.

Should I use one color or stripes?

Stripes are great for using up stash yarn and hide the cost of buying one huge quantity. Just match total yardage per color section using the calculator.
